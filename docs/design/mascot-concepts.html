Earth Jukebox mascot: Echo the Juke-Bot

Echo, the guide, is a small friendly robot with an LED visor helmet, headphones, a chest screen (equaliser bars and a mini globe), gloves and air-booster boots. Our own design, drawn as SVG by the team; no antenna, no NASA insignia. Echo is a program, not a person. This page is rendered from the real component (src/components/Mascot.tsx). The earlier astronaut concepts are in the git history.

Five visor expressions

Each differs by shape, not only colour (WCAG 1.4.1). Set with the expression prop; the pose picks one if it is not set.

happy

Two arc eyes and a smile. The default.

searching

A radar line that sweeps, and three query dots.

eq_active

Five wave bars. Shown while the guide talks.

alert

An orange warning triangle with "!".

resting

Two closed-eye lines, no mouth.

Poses (used by the voice guide)

Each state is also in text. The mascot is decoration and only repeats what the text says.

Idle

Text: "Voice guide is ready"

Listening

Ear-cup LEDs glow, waves come in

Talking

EQ bars in the visor; music is ducked

Thinking

Radar line and thought dots

Four motion states

Shown here as stills, which is also what people with reduced motion see. The animations only run under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, and nothing flashes more than 3 times a second.

idle

Floats about 4 px every 4 s.

boosting

Forward tilt of 15 degrees; flames scale with thrust.

retro

Boots tilt back and burn forward for about 400 ms.

pointing

The right arm swings out. No loop.

Notes

  • Decoration only: aria-hidden="true" on the drawing.
  • Colours are --mascot-* CSS variables with these values as fallbacks, so a data set can recolour the accent.
  • Not like: WALL-E (eyes on stalks), EVE (glossy egg), R2-D2 (dome on a barrel), BB-8 (ball), Baymax (soft inflatable), Astro Boy (blue-and-white jet-boot boy).
