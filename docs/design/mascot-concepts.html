Earth Jukebox mascot: three directions

An astronaut who listens to Earth. Original design in the app's Material You colours. It borrows the friendly, rounded feel of the my.t Learning Hub robot, not its shape, colours or branding.

A. "Echo": astronaut with headphones and Earth in the visor

Full body. Earth is reflected in the dark visor, and the glowing eyes and smile sit on top of it. Big headphones say "sound" at a glance. The chest badge is a tiny equaliser.

B. "Orbit": chibi helmet with a ring of sound

Mostly head, like a sticker. A tilted ring goes round the helmet like a planet's ring, and a small Earth rides on it. Softer and more playful; happy arc eyes.

C. "Sonny": the avatar for small sizes

Head only, inside a round badge, for the voice guide button, the panel header and the browser tab. Checked at 96, 48, 32 and 24 px.

Echo's states (for the voice guide)

Each state must also be in text for screen readers. The mascot is decoration and only repeats what the text says.

Notes

  • Decoration only: aria-hidden="true" on the drawing; the guide's state stays in text.
