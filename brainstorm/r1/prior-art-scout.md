TL;DR: TouchingSpace is real and our plan cites it correctly, but it has no time dimension, so "time under your finger" is the one clearly new piece.
No NASA Earth or EIC sonification project found; the gap looks open.

Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only (Exa and Firecrawl not in my tools). [verified] = fetched by me; [unverified] = snippet or unopened.

Negative finding: no NASA Earth or EIC sonification project, and no earth.gov audio description, found. Absence is not proof.

## Proposal to upgrade the plan

Make "time" the headline. TouchingSpace has proximity and boundary cues on a static map, so we add a per-place time series under the finger (proximity hum keyed to change between two dates). Borrow its cues, cite both IDs, and test time mode with a 14-person style study.

## Radical alternative

"Tune your own Earth": the blind user picks the mapping (which colour is high, which pitch range) and hears the same frame under two mappings. I found no tool where blind users author the rules. This answers the "sonify frames, not NASA numbers" risk.

## One thing to cut

Cut "touch the planet" as a headline. TouchingSpace already does its core pattern. Keep it as a 30-second demo beat on a flat map of one gold story.

Weakest point of my own findings: most finds are [unverified] snippets; only the two TouchingSpace papers were opened. Arcand and Chandra claims need a fetch before judge-facing use.
