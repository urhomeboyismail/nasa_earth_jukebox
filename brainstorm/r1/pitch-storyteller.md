# R1 Pitch Storyteller

TL;DR: The pitch wins if the judge's own eyes are taken away for 20 seconds and the data is still understood. Cut "any visualization, live" as the headline; sell one story plus a blindfold moment.

## 1. Upgrade: "Close your eyes" (the blindfold open)

**10-year-old sentence:** "It lets blind people hear a NASA map, and find the spot where the ice melted most, with one finger."

**5-second hook:** Judge is handed headphones and a phone, told "close your eyes". No talk. A low hum, then a thinning shimmer for Arctic ice, 1979 on. A voice: "Find where the ice is gone." They slide a finger; a pull sound leads them; ping. They open their eyes and see the same spot on the EIC frame. Heard first, seen second.

**Why better:** the plan opens on a passive black-screen symphony; the blindfold makes the judge the user.

**30 s video (subtitled):** 0-5 s black screen, subtitle "Priya cannot see this map." [assumption: persona]. 5-15 s her finger finds the ice loss, tester reaction on camera. 15-25 s sighted view: frame and sound in sync. 25-30 s one cited number plus the one-liner. Needs a real tester quote; no quote, no video.

**Local angle (Mauritius):** make the second story the Indian Ocean around Mauritius (sea-level or upwelling), with the home beacon as a soft ping on the island. End line: "Ice melts far away. The sea rises here." [unverified: data scout to confirm a NASA dataset and number].

**7 slides:** problem; blindfold test; frame to note; touch and find; truth rules; NASA data and AI disclosure; Mauritius and next steps.

## 2. Radical alternative: "The Planet's Radio"

No shelf, no agents on stage. One dial turns through time and the Earth's sound changes. One place (Mauritius coast), one question: "Is my sea rising?" Sentence: "Hold the ocean to your ear and hear 30 years." Easier to feel than a jukebox with Side A/B. It drops "any EIC visualization" and looks thin on scale, so use it as the fallback demo if the build slips.

## 3. Cut

**Cut "judge names any visualization live" as the headline.** It is the riskiest beat, and one wrong or silent result ends the room's trust. Keep it as an optional encore on pre-tested items; if it fails, say "unsupported, here is why", which shows honesty. Also cut Earth Symphony from the demo; it is passive and already first to cut.

## Emotion vs evidence

Emotion: the blindfold, the tester's quote, the island ping. Evidence: one number matched to NASA's published value, with the error shown on a slide. Never narrate emotion; the data carries it.

Weakest point of my own proposal: the blindfold moment depends on touch working with TalkBack on, which is unproven; if it fails, the opening switches to keyboard or button exploration and loses its punch. Also the tester quote is not yet booked.
