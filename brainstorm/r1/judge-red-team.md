# Round 1 — Judge / Red Team (independent)

TL;DR: The plan is a feature buffet; judges reward one unforgettable proof that a blind person learned something true from NASA data. Fatal flaw: the headline (live mode, any EIC viz) is the least reliable part.

## Likely fatal flaw
"Name any visualization" live, chosen by a judge. Legend reading on an unseen video is fragile `[assumption]`. If it fails, judges hear wrong sound, which breaks Validity and "never fake data". Also, ten features plus two agent systems means nothing is polished.

## 1. Upgrade: a measured outcome as centrepiece
Run a 15-minute test with 2-3 blind or low-vision users in Mauritius. Record one number ("X of Y found the hotspot unaided in under 30 s") and one permissioned quote. Put it on slide 1 and in the video. Most teams show a demo; almost none show a user result. It lifts Influence, Validity, Presentation and Local Impact. Add a "what the sound hides" limitations slide; honest limits impress scientists. Novelty answer, one sentence: time series on real EIC frames, grounded talk, tested with blind users, unlike TouchingSpace or Chandra.

## 2. Radical alternative: "Same Data, Two Ears"
One gold story only (Arctic ice or temperature). The judge hears the same year twice: strict data sonification (Side B) and musical (Side A), then is asked "which would you trust?" The data-versus-musicality trade-off the brief names becomes the product. Frames drive both; touch-to-explore on one image. No live mode; the Narrator answers from a pre-written fact sheet. One deep, accessible, defended thing.

## 3. Cut
- Earth Symphony, Your Year, share, haptic language (no iOS support).
- Live mode as headline. Keep as a pre-tested bonus; never let a judge pick the topic on stage.
- Navigator + Narrator + Composer in the demo path: each hop adds latency and failure. Pre-compose.

## Questions I would ask in Q&A
Which dataset, layer ID and date range? What does silence mean? Who tested it and what did they say? What does the musical scale hide? Does touch work with TalkBack on, on a real phone?

Weakest point of my own review: the user test assumes blind testers are reachable in time, and the demo-format advice rests on 2025 rules.
