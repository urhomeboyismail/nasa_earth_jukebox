## Rule check (all)
No NASA logos; disclose AI use; demo must fit 30 s or 7 slides, so Options 1 and 2 demo easiest.

## GROUPTHINK DUTY

**Keep "judge names any EIC visualization live".** It is the only moment that proves the tool is not pre-baked. The brief says users "select" a visualization; a judge picking one is the strongest selection test, and the room remembers it. A curated shelf looks like a playlist, which is allowed but ordinary. Cheap version: live mode limited to GIBS layers listed on the shelf, with a printed fallback list judges pick from. Real fail mode is a live crash, which is a risk, not a reason to cut. Verdict: the case survives as a pick-from-a-menu live moment (demo as a scripted bonus, never the pitch headline), not as free-text any-video CV. Free-text fails on Validity and Feasibility.

**Keep the Earth Symphony.** Judges reward a finale; Best Storytelling and Most Inspirational need an arc. Joining the three stories in a final piece makes the three chapters one narrative and shows the same rules scale. It also tests the "at most two layers" rule honestly: a symphony would break it. Verdict: the case mostly fails. It breaks the two-layer rule, hides data under music, and costs hours. Survives only as a 20-second sequential medley (story A, then B, no overlap) if the first stories finish early. Cut by default.

## Groupthink duty 2: against Option 1

Case against: Option 1 wins a room of reviewers, not a judging table. Judges saw 50 projects; a two-mappings A/B on one sea-ice story is the most "map colours to notes" project of all, just with better manners. Influence 3 is honest: one Arctic story, one blind student, no reason to care outside the demo. Creativity 4 is generous; Side A/B and a home pitch are craft, not a new idea. Its Presentation 5 depends on a blind-test number that may never exist. "Selecting a visualization" is thin: the user picks nothing.

Option 3 answers all three. Place x year is the thing existing touch maps cannot do (static, no time) [verified: arxiv.org/abs/2609.36404]. It raises Influence (any place, any person) and Creativity (new interaction, not new palette). Option 4 raises Influence and Local Impact: a Mauritian student hearing her own sea is the Most Inspirational moment no Arctic story can match. A 3+4 hybrid (Planet Dial with Mauritius preloaded) would beat Option 1 on Influence and Creativity, and tie on Relevance.

Verdict: the case partly survives. It fails on Feasibility and Validity: Option 3 needs a regional grid pipeline, and Option 4's SST data is Amber and unverified, so a decision cannot rest on it. Keep Option 1 as the core.

The ONE element to absorb: Option 4's Mauritius close. After the Arctic story, one "here" step: same two-mappings player, same GIBS pipeline, one Mauritius-region layer, only if its data is verified on day 1; else cut silently.
