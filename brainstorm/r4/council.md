# Council: Option 1 vs Option 4 (or merge)

TL;DR: All four voices pick Option 1 as the core and reject the coast duet. Three of four fold in a gated Mauritius SST chapter; the Skeptic dissents: Option 1 alone, Mauritius only as a stretch epilogue.
Question: "Which of these two do we build, or how do we merge them?" Voices: Architect = facilitator (written first, scoreboard §8); Skeptic, Pragmatist, Critic = fresh subagents given only the question, scoreboard §1–7 and CLAUDE.md constraints.

**Architect:** Option 1 core, with Option 4 merged as its closing "here" chapter (one Mauritius SST layer, same player and pipeline). Gate it on a day-1 data check; the coast duet is a stretch.
Why: Option 1 wins four of seven criteria and passes every gate; Option 4's place/emotion is cheap on the same engine, but its Validity would sink it as the core.

**Skeptic:** Option 1 is the whole product. No promised Mauritius chapter: it is a stretch epilogue, only if the day-1 check passes and the core is done. Fix "selecting a visualization" with a small fixed menu of pre-tested GIBS layers instead.
Why: Option 4 is carried by one feature (ICE 6.0) and its duet is padding (2.7); a "merge" imports its weak Validity into the pitch. Local Impact can be told in one spoken line.

**Pragmatist:** Option 1 core plus one Mauritius SST chapter (the time dial), gated on day 1; cut the duet. Build the Mauritius chapter **first**: it has the only hard one-hour pass/fail test.
Why: the merge reuses one pipeline (+1 layer), covers Option 1's weak Influence and "select" gaps, and keeps Local Impact in play; if the tile fails, fall back without re-planning.

**Critic:** Option 1 core plus a cheap, data-verified Mauritius SST close; no duet, and no Local Impact claim built on it. Treat the NSIDC cross-check as a day-1 **kill test**.
Why: if pixel-count extent misses NSIDC, "frames are the foundation" collapses; a second layer brings a second colormap, gaps and wording. Cut it the moment the check is Red.

### Verdict

- **Consensus (4/4):** Option 1 ("One Story, Two Ears") is the core. Cut the coast duet. Run two day-1 kill tests: (1) MODIS ice-pixel extent vs NSIDC September extent; (2) MUR SST tile covers Mauritius with a usable range. Book a real blind tester now: it is a booking problem, not a build problem, and without it Accessibility 4.8 is a self-score.
- **Strongest dissent (Skeptic):** don't merge. Any Mauritius chapter imports Option 4's weak Validity into the pitch; spend the effort on a small GIBS layer menu that answers "select a visualization" directly. *Kept visible; partly adopted:* the GIBS menu (3 pre-tested layers) moves from stretch into the core as the "choose" step, sized down.
- **Premise check:** yes. The Skeptic and Critic both said the real question isn't 1-vs-4. It's "Is Option 1's evidence real?" (the tester and the NSIDC match). The Critic added that a 30 s demo shows one beat; pick it now: **the silence map** (sound stops where data is missing; voice and text say "no data, not zero").
- **Change to the Architect position:** two voices (Pragmatist, Critic) pushed the Mauritius chapter's role around: build it first as a fast data probe, and make it the close only if it passes. The Architect accepts this. The Skeptic's menu point is accepted. The Architect's "merge" stands, but as **gated**, not promised.
- **Recommendation:** **Option 1 core + gated Mauritius SST close.**
  - Hour 0–6: both kill tests.
  - Core: Side A/B switch, silence map, reference tone on demand, Year slider, cited static Q&A, a small 3-layer GIBS "choose" menu, and a "what the sound hides" scene.
  - Demo built around the silence-map beat. The Mauritius SST dial closes the video only if its test passes.
  - Fallbacks: if the ice test fails, SST becomes the main story and ice moves to the limits scene. If the Mauritius test fails, cut it with no re-plan.
