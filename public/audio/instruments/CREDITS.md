# Recorded instruments

The notes in these folders come from the **FluidR3_GM** SoundFont by Frank Wen, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (CC BY 3.0).

They were rendered one MP3 a note by [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
(commit `044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef`) and cut down to a note every minor third over the range each
music style uses, by `scripts/fetch_instruments.mjs`. The notes are unchanged otherwise.

Folders: `marimba`, `vibraphone`, `acoustic_grand_piano`, `string_ensemble_1`, `cello`, `pizzicato_strings`,
`orchestral_harp`, `flute`, `acoustic_bass`, `tubular_bells`. Each file is named after its note (`Eb4.mp3`, middle C is `C4`).

The app credits the SoundFont on its About page.
